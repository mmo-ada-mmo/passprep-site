Privacy Policy

PassPrep · Last updated: 8 November 2026

PassPrep ("the app") is designed to protect your privacy. We do not collect, track, sell, or share your personal information. You do not need an account to use the app.

Information we collect

None. PassPrep has no user accounts, no login, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or device identifiers.

Data stored on your device

Your study data — onboarding choices (such as your exam and target date), practice history, answers, and readiness progress — is stored only on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. Deleting the app removes this data.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment information. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third parties

PassPrep does not send your personal information to any third party. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics services.

Children's privacy

PassPrep is intended for adults preparing for a professional licensing exam and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data lives on your device, you are in full control: delete the app to erase all locally stored study data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email passprep.support@gmail.com.
