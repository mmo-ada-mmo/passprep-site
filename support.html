Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open PassPrep, go to the paywall or Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone: open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select PassPrep, and change or cancel there. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

How does the free trial work?

Pro Monthly and Pro Quarterly include a 1-week free trial. If you don't cancel before the trial ends, it converts to a paid subscription at the listed price. You can cancel any time during the trial from your Apple subscription settings.

What does the app cover?

PassPrep currently focuses on the California Real Estate Salesperson licensing exam: adaptive practice, per-question explanations, mock exams, readiness scoring, and a personalized study plan.

Is my data private?

Yes. All study data stays on your device and is never collected or shared. See our Privacy Policy.
